## Arduino and the XIAO

* **[Arduino IDE](https://www.arduino.cc/en/software):** editing and uploading device programs.
* **[Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3 guide](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/xiao_esp32s3_getting_started/):** board overview, pins and setup. Our examples use the **esp32 board package 2.0.17**.

## Edge Impulse and on-device AI

* **[Edge Impulse documentation](https://docs.edgeimpulse.com/):** model training, evaluation and deployment.
* **[Seeed Studio: XIAO ESP32S3 image classification](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/tinyml_course_Image_classification_project/)** and Marcelo Rovai, **[*XIAO: Big Power, Small Board*, chapter 4.4](https://mjrovai.github.io/XIAO_Big_Power_Small_Board-ebook/chapter_4-4.html):** deploying an image classifier on the XIAO.
* **[SenseCraft Web Toolkit](https://seeed-studio.github.io/SenseCraft-Web-Toolkit/):** live preview of a classification model in the browser.

## Image sources

* **[Openverse API](https://api.openverse.org/v1/):** openly licensed images, with creator and licence for each result. Registration steps come with the Day 2 materials; keep the credentials out of notebooks and shared files.
* **[SerpAPI Google Images API](https://serpapi.com/google-images-api):** Google Images results. Licences are unknown: use them for training only, never in the films.
* **[Wikimedia Commons image metadata](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Imageinfo/en):** an alternative source of image URLs and metadata.
* **[Creative Commons licences](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/):** what each licence allows and requires.

## Sensors and fabrication

* **[Grove GSR sensor](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/Grove-GSR_Sensor/):** specification and calibration of the optional skin-response sensor.
* **[Rhino scripting](https://www.rhino3d.com/features/developer/scripting/):** reference for AI-assisted design and enclosure modelling.
